Privacy

This site is run by Perotti Consulting, the business of Daniele Perotti in Amsterdam (KVK 76624102).

What the site records

The site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

A visitor counter, Umami, records each visit: the page, the site you came from, your browser and type of device, your country, and which parts of the page you opened. It keeps no name and no IP address, and it cannot recognise you on a later day. I use it to see which parts of the page are read.

The site is hosted by GitHub, which keeps technical logs of visits, including IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

When you write to me

I keep your message and your email address to reply and to follow up on our conversation. My mail runs on Google Workspace. I do not sell or pass on your details.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it: connect [at] danieleperotti [dot] com. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
